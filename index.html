What a link looks like

One beetle. Many names.

The stag beetle lives in old oak and beech woods, and every database knows it by a different name. VERDANT writes down that they mean the same animal, as a three-word sentence. Tap a name.

subjectn2k:species_lucanus_cervus
verbskos:exactMatch
objectobo:NCBITaxon_41109
